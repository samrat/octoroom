Octoroom is a toolkit for building realtime AI voice and vision agents—from 1-on-1 calls to multi-party rooms—directly inside your Elixir/Phoenix application, backed by [Cloudflare Realtime SFU](https://developers.cloudflare.com/realtime/sfu/).

Cloudflare carries the WebRTC media while your Phoenix app owns room state, authentication, routing policies, and server-side workers.
